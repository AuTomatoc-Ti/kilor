# Kilor Optative — Wishes, Hopes & Desires

Kilor uses a single content root `halise` ("hope, wish") to express three irrealis-desire moods. `halise` is an **open-class content root** (3 syllables), not a closed-class particle — it follows standard tone patterns for 3+ syllable words (see `0-foundation/tone-prosody.md` §II-A). Colour-emotion particles provide finer emotional control.

## I. Morphological Forms

| Category | Form | Last-3 Pattern | Meaning |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Noun** | `hajlise` | H(j)→M→L | hope (the feeling) |
| **Verb** | `havlise` | L(v)→H→M | to hope, to wish |
| **Adjective** | `halijse` | M→H(j)→H | hopeful |
| **Adverb** | `halivse` | M→L(v)→M | hopefully |

---

## II. Syntax

### A. Verb Form (`havlise`) — Primary Usage

`havlise` occupies the verb slot in the SOV template. Three patterns disambiguate the mood:

#### 1. Desiderative — Subject + `havlise` + `kus` + clause

| Kilor | Meaning |
|:---|:---|
| `ki havlise kus ti kau` | "I hope that you come" |
| `si havlise kus hupli hup nar` | "He hopes that rain does not fall" |

#### 2. Optative — Impersonal `havlise` (no subject)

No subject. The wished-for clause follows directly without `kus` (parallels bare juxtaposition for reported speech, `2-predication/subordination.md` §V):

| Kilor | Meaning |
|:---|:---|
| `havlise hupli hup` | "May rain fall" |
| `havlise fora fora nar` | "May the fire not burn" |

#### 3. Benedictive — `havlise` + 2nd/3rd person target

| Kilor | Meaning |
|:---|:---|
| `havlise ti gor roi os` | "May you have a good day" |
| `havlise si losto res` | "May he/she be happy" |

### B. Noun Form (`hajlise`) — The Feeling of Hope

| Kilor | Meaning |
|:---|:---|
| `ki e-hajlise os` | "I have a warm hope" |
| `a-hajlise ralis res` | "A burning hope is great" |

## III. Interaction with Emotional Particles

Emotional particles layer nuance onto optative expressions (see `3-subsystems/colour-emotion.md`):

| Kilor | Meaning |
|:---|:---|
| `e, ki havlise kus ti kau` | "(With joy,) I hope that you come" |
| `i, havlise si kau` | "(With sadness,) may he come" |
| `u, havlise ti gor roi` | "(With calm,) may you have a good day" |

## IV. Summary Table

| Mood | Construction | Example |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Desiderative** | Subject + `havlise` + `kus` + clause | `ki havlise kus ti kau` |
| **Optative** | `havlise` + clause (no subject) | `havlise hupli hup` |
| **Benedictive** | `havlise` + 2nd/3rd person target | `havlise ti gor roi os` |
| **Hortative** | `sor` (sentence-final — see `imperatives.md`) | `ki taka sor` |
