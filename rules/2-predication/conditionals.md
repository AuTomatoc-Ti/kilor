# Kilor Conditionals

**Module:** Conditional & Consequential Clauses
**Status:** Canonical
**Last updated:** 2026-07-11
**Version:** 1.1.0
**Depends on:** `0-foundation/grammar-syntax.md`, `2-predication/subordination.md`
**Interacts with:** `2-predication/negation.md` (nar scoping), `3-subsystems/colour-emotion.md` (emotional particles on clauses)

---

Kilor expresses conditionals through two closed-class particles (see `0-foundation/grammar-syntax.md` §IV-C):

| Particle | Meaning | Type |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `li` | if | Conditional subordinator |
| `bam` | then / so / consequently | Consequential particle (便/就) |

`bam` also serves as a general consequential connector ("X happened, so Y happened").

---

## I. Conditional Constructions

### A. Pattern 1 — Correlative (if…then)

**Structure:** `li [condition], bam [result]`

| Kilor | Meaning |
|:---|:---|
| `li ti kau, bam ki losto res` | "If you come, then I am happy" |
| `li hupli hup, bam ki kau nar` | "If rain falls, then I do not come" |

### B. Pattern 2 — Postposed Condition (no `bam`)

**Structure:** `[result] li [condition]`

| Kilor | Meaning |
|:---|:---|
| `ki losto res li ti kau` | "I am happy if you come" |

When the condition is postposed, `bam` is not used.

---

## II. `bam` as General Consequential (便/就)

`bam` may appear without `li` to mark that the second clause follows naturally from the first:

| Kilor | Meaning |
|:---|:---|
| `si rilda, bam ki kau` | "He spoke, then I came" |
| `ti bau taka, bam thep` | "You eat bread, then (you) sleep" |

This is not a temporal sequence marker — for that, use bare juxtaposition or `ei` (see `2-predication/clause-combining.md`).

---

## III. Interaction with Other Systems

### A. Negation

`nar` scopes over the clause it appears in:

| Kilor | Meaning |
|:---|:---|
| `li ti kau nar, bam ki hik res` | "If you do not come, then I am sad" |
| `li ti kau, bam ki hik res nar` | "If you come, then I am not sad" |

### B. Emotional Particles

Emotional particles may appear on either clause independently (see `3-subsystems/colour-emotion.md`):

> `e, li ti kau, bam ki losto res` — "(With joy,) if you come, then I am happy"

### C. Aspect

Aspect particles operate within each clause independently (see `3-subsystems/aspect.md`):

> `li ti kau ger, bam ki taka ger` — "If you have come, then I have eaten"
