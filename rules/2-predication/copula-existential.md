## I. Core Philosophy

Kilor distinguishes two relational verbs:

| Verb | Function | Chinese Parallel |
|---|---|---|
| **`res`** | Copula — identity, attribution (X **is** Y) | 是 |
| **`ero`** | Existential & Possession — existence (**there is**) and having (**have**) | 有 |

This split reflects a philosophical stance: **being something** and **having something** are fundamentally different relations. Identity is abstract and static (White: void/formless). Existence and possession are dynamic and localised (the Brown friction between self and world).

`ero` unifies existence and possession — in Kilor, "fire exists" and "I have fire" are the same grammatical operation. Possession is existence brought into one's sphere.

## II. Verb Forms

### A. `res` — Copula

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| **Root** | `res` |
| **Syllables** | 1 |
| **Category** | Verb (closed-class function word) |
| **Tone** | Toneless (flat mid-tone) — follows 1-syllable toneless rule |
| **Class** | Closed-class — exempt from the open-root `-s` constraint (see `0-foundation/grammar-syntax.md` §IV-C) |

`res` expresses:

1. **Predicate adjective:** "X is warm/big/good"
2. **Predicate nominal:** "X is a person/fire/star"

### B. `ero` — Existential & Possession

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| **Root** | `ero` |
| **Syllables** | 2 |
| **Category** | Verb (closed-class function word) |
| **Tone** | Toneless (flat mid-tone) — follows 2-syllable toneless rule |
| **Class** | Closed-class — exempt from the open-root `-s` constraint (see `0-foundation/grammar-syntax.md` §IV-C) |

`ero` expresses:

1. **Existential:** "There is X" / "X exists"
2. **Possession:** "Y has X" (same structure: possessor — possessed — `ero`)

## III. Word Order

Both verbs follow the standard **SOV** word order:

```
Subject — Complement — Verb
```

### A. Predicate Adjective

> `a-fora wem res.`
> (The fire warm is.)
> "The fire is warm."

> `fora wem res.`
> (Fire warm is.)
> "Fire is warm." (generic)

### B. Predicate Nominal

> `ki maeha res.`
> (I person am.)
> "I am a person."

> `si a-fora res.`
> (It the-fire is.)
> "It is the fire."

### C. Existential

> `fora ero.`
> (Fire exists.)
> "There is fire." / "Fire exists."

> `maeha ero.`
> (Person exists.)
> "There is a person."

### D. Possession

> `ki fora ero.`
> (I fire have.)
> "I have fire."

> `si lira ero.`
> (He/she water has.)
> "He/she has water."

## IV. Interaction with Negation

### A. Copula Negation

`nar` follows the copula, scoping over the predication:

> `fora wem res nar.`
> "Fire is not warm."

> `ki maeha res nar.`
> "I am not a person."

### B. Existential Negation

`nar` follows `ero`, negating existence:

> `fora ero nar.`
> "There is no fire." / "Fire does not exist."

### C. Possession Negation

`nar` follows `ero`, negating possession:

> `ki fora ero nar.`
> "I do not have fire."

### D. Constituent Negation

As with all verbs (see `2-predication/negation.md` §II-B), `nar` placed before the verb negates only the preceding constituent:

> `ki nar fora ero.`
> "Not I have fire." (someone else does)

> `ki fora nar ero.`
> "I not-fire have." (I have something else)

## V. Interaction with Interrogatives

### A. Yes/No Questions

Sentence-final `iu` as usual (see `2-predication/interrogative.md` §IV):

> `fora wem res iu?`
> "Is fire warm?"

> `ki maeha res iu?`
> "Am I a person?"

> `fora ero iu?`
> "Is there fire?"

> `ki fora ero iu?`
> "Do I have fire?"

### B. Wh-Questions

Question words are fronted as usual, including with copula/existential:

> `ewei wem res iu?`
> (What warm is?)
> "What is warm?"

> `iwei fora ero?`
> (Where fire exists?)
> "Where is there fire?"

> `aewei fora ero?`
> (Who fire has?)
> "Who has fire?"

## VI. Interaction with Case Marking

### A. Copula — Both Arguments Nominative

The copula is a **linking verb** — neither argument is an object. Both subject and complement are in the **Nominative** (unmarked). No Accusative suffix is used.

> ✅ `ki maeha res.` (I person am.)
> ❌ `ki maehani res.` (person in Accusative — invalid with copula)

### B. Existential & Possession — Standard Case Rules Apply

Possession with `ero` uses standard SOV case marking:

> `ki forani ero.` (I fire-ACC have.) — Accusative optional in SOV speech, mandatory in formal/non-SOV

---

## VII. Zero-Copula in Ambient & Weather Statements

For statements about ambient conditions (weather, temperature, light level), the copula **may** be omitted in everyday speech. This is a stylistic option, not a grammatical requirement.

> `wem.` = "It's warm." (lit. "warm.")
> `wem res.` = same meaning, fully grammatical

> `my.` = "It's dark."
> `my res.` = same meaning

This is the **only** context where zero-copula is permitted. Predicate nominals and possession always require their verbs.
