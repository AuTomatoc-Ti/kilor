## I. Constraint Checklist

Quick reference. Verify each root against these before writing it down.

### Phoneme Inventory

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Vowels (7)** | `a` /ɑ/, `e` /ɛ/, `i` /i/, `o` /ɔ/, `u` /u/, `y` /y/, `ae` /æ/ |
| **Diphthongs (7)** | `ai`, `au`, `ei`, `eu`, `iu`, `oi`, `ou` |
| **Core consonants (15)** | `p`, `b`, `m`, `f`, `w`, `t`, `d`, `n`, `s`, `l`, `r`, `c` (/ts/), `k`, `g`, `h` |
| **`j` / `v`** | ❌ **Never** in a root. Reserved for tone only. |
| **Start-only onsets (8)** | `sh`, `ch`, `th`, `sl`, `kl`, `tl`, `bl`, `ml` — word-initial only, cannot appear in mono-word compounds |
| **Ending-only consonants (2)** | `ng`, `x` — word-final only, cannot appear in mono-word compounds |

### Syllable Templates

Every syllable must be one of: **CV**, **CVC**, **VC**, **V**

- ❌ No consonant clusters (no CCV, CVCC, etc.)
- ✅ Diphthong counts as one V nucleus

### Length & `-s`

| Constraint |
|---|
| Roots must be **1–5 syllables** long |
| 1-syllable and 2-syllable roots **must not** end in `s` natively (`-s` is reserved as the derivational modifier suffix) |
| 3+ syllable roots **may** end in `s` natively |

### Tone Markers

- **Do not** include `j` or `v` in any root. Tone will be applied externally after category assignment.
- The root form you write is the bare segmental spelling only.
- Existing tone-marked forms already in the codebase (e.g., `fojra`, `lujmi`, `tavka`) are included as-is for reference — those are the finished noun/verb forms, not bare roots.

## III. How to Fill This In

1. **Write the bare root form** — no `j`, no `v`, no `-s`. Just the segmental spelling.
2. **Count syllables** — each vowel or diphthong nucleus = 1 syllable.
3. **Notes (optional)** — any nuance, alternative meaning, cultural context, or connection to the 7 dual-concepts.

### What happens next (I'll do this):

- **Validate** each root against the phonotactic constraints in §I. Flag any violations.
- **Assign a category** (Noun / Verb / Adjective / Adverb) for each root.
- **Generate the 4-form expansion**: bare root → Noun form (with tone markers), Verb form, Adjective form (Noun + `-s` or 3+ syl tone pattern), Adverb form (Verb + `-s` or 3+ syl tone pattern).
- **Suggest default colour prefixes (共識)** for noun roots based on the 7-Question Filter.
