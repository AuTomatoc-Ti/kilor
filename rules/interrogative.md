# Kilor Interrogative — Question Formation

**Module:** Wh-Questions & Yes/No Questions
**Status:** Canonical

---

## I. Core Root

The root **`wei`** carries the core meaning of "question / unknown / what". It is a **1-syllable word** and follows the standard 1-syllable tone rule: **toneless** (flat mid-tone). See `tone-prosody.md` §II-C.

On its own, `wei` can be used as a general expression of uncertainty or questioning (like "huh?" or "what?").

---

## II. Wh-Questions — Colour Prefix + `wei`

Question words are formed by attaching a **colour prefix** to the root `wei`. Each prefix maps to a specific question word, drawing on the philosophical dual-concept of that colour.

| Colour | Prefix | Question Word | Philosophy Link |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Brown** | `ae-` | **Who?** | 自我 (Ego) / 他者 (Other) — personhood and identity |
| **Red** | `a-` | **Which?** | 犧牲 (Sacrifice) / 覺悟 (Awakening) — choosing between options |
| **Yellow** | `e-` | **What?** | 課題 (Quest) / 意義 (Meaning) — the thing sought or defined |
| **Blue** | `i-` | **Where?** | 孤離 (Solitude) / 並存 (Coexistence) — space and location |
| **White** | `o-` | **When?** | 遺憾 (Regret) / 可能性 (Possibility) — time and its passage |
| **Green** | `u-` | **How?** | 救贖 (Salvation) / 信念 (Faith) — method, path, process |
| **Black** | `y-` | **Why?** | 敬畏 (Reverence) / 探求 (Exploration) — reason and deeper cause |

### A. Tone

The prefixed question words are **2-syllable** and follow the standard **noun pattern** H(`j`)→L:

| Form | Tone | Spelling |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `a-wei` | H(`j`)→L | `awej` |
| `e-wei` | H(`j`)→L | `ewej` |
| `i-wei` | H(`j`)→L | `iwej` |
| `o-wei` | H(`j`)→L | `owej` |
| `u-wei` | H(`j`)→L | `uwej` |
| `y-wei` | H(`j`)→L | `ywej` |
| `ae-wei` | H(`j`)→L | `aewej` |

### B. Colour Prefix Rules

The colour prefix follows the standard rules from `nouns-colour-prefix.md`:
- It is an external proclitic, pronounced flat mid-tone
- It does **not** trigger vowel harmony on the root
- The tone marker `j` is placed on the **first syllable** of the 2-syllable form

---

## III. Word Order — Fronting

All question words are **fronted** to the **beginning of the sentence**, regardless of their grammatical role. This is a strict rule — question words cannot remain in situ.

> **Example (subject question):** `awej a-kojra tavka?` (Which [one] eats the fire?)
> **Example (object question):** `ewej ti tavka?` (What do you eat?)
> **Example (location question):** `iwej a-kojra?` (Where is the fire?)
> **Example (reason question):** `ywej ti a-kojra tavka?` (Why do you eat fire?)

---

## IV. Yes/No Questions — `iu` Particle

Yes/no (polar) questions are formed by appending the affirmative particle **`iu`** to the end of a declarative sentence. The intonation rises on `iu`.

> **Statement:** `ti a-kojra tavka.` (You eat fire.)
> **Question:** `ti a-kojra tavka iu?` (Do you eat fire?)

### A. Answering

Yes/no questions are answered using the standalone particles from `negation.md`:

> **Q:** `ti a-kojra tavka iu?`
> **A:** `iu.` (Yes.) / `na.` (No.)

## V. Summary

| Question Type | Mechanism | Example |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Who?** | `ae-wei` (fronted) | `aewej a-kojra tavka?` |
| **Which?** | `a-wei` (fronted) | `awej a-kojra tavka?` |
| **What?** | `e-wei` (fronted) | `ewej ti tavka?` |
| **Where?** | `i-wei` (fronted) | `iwej a-kojra?` |
| **When?** | `o-wei` (fronted) | `owej ti tavka?` |
| **How?** | `u-wei` (fronted) | `uwej ti tavka?` |
| **Why?** | `y-wei` (fronted) | `ywej ti a-kojra tavka?` |
| **Yes/No?** | `iu` (sentence-final) | `ti a-kojra tavka iu?` |
